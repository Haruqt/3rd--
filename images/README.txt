Drop your photos here using these exact filenames:

baby-photo.jpg   - a beautiful photo of her
lovebirds.jpg    - a sweet, romantic photo of the two of you together
                   (cuddling, hugging, smiling at each other — not
                   tied to any specific date, just you two being sweet)
babycake.jpg     - cute or funny photo
hug.jpg          - cute hug photo
kiss.jpg         - cute kiss photo
smile.jpg        - photo of her smiling
funny.jpg        - funny photo or meme
selos.jpg        - funny jealous-themed photo or meme
clingy.jpg       - cute couple / clingy photo
coupon.jpg       - digital coupon graphic
secret.jpg       - mystery / question-mark image
random-baby.jpg  - any random cute/funny/candid photo of her — the
                   surprise is that she won't know which one it'll be
final.jpg        - the special final prize photo

Until a file exists, the game shows a pretty gradient circle with the
prize's initial instead of a broken image, so it's safe to ship without them.
